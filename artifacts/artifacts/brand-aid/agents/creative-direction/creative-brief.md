# Creative Direction Brief — B4U.bet

## Visual territory

- **Mood:** Stadium lights at dusk meets data center — premium, not neon casino
- **References:** The Athletic typography discipline + FiveThirtyEight data clarity + BR football passion
- **Avoid:** Slot machine gradients, money rain, aggressive red/green "win" cues

## Design principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — dados legíveis primeiro
2. **Dark-first mobile** — OLED-friendly, high contrast WCAG AA
3. **Motion:** subtle — score tickers, não slot spins
4. **Photography:** atletas em contexto, nunca celebrando dinheiro

## Logo direction

- Abstração de **radar/sinal** + tipografia wordmark "B4U" forte; ".bet" secundário e discreto
- Deve funcionar em favicon 16px e hero 1200px

## Copy tone

- PT-BR direto, sem gíria de tipster
- EN export: concise, authoritative
